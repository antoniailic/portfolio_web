01 Intro

Translate a clean, tangible brand, focused on the materials of its fabrics, without losing its texture.

Context

Lebor Gabala is a Barcelona knitwear label founded in 1989 by Maite Muñoz. Its name comes from Lebor Gabála Érenn, a book of Irish mythology, chosen for the bond between Ireland and the wool tradition. The brand designs for durability: pieces meant to last and to mix with garments from earlier collections.

Challenge & role

Content had to feel as considered as the garments: elegant, tactile and unhurried. I built the AW26 Instagram content plan, designed the graphic system for stories and posts, and supported the campaign shoot on set.
